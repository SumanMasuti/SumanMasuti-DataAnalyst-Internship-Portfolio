## 📌 Internship Executive Summary

This portfolio showcases end-to-end data analytics capabilities applied to real-world transactional and demographic data. The internship covered the full analytics lifecycle across five key tasks:

1. **Task 1: Data Immersion & Wrangling** — Cleaned raw data, handled missing values, standardized formats, and engineered demographic features (`Age_Group`).
2. **Task 2: Exploratory Data Analysis & Business Intelligence** — Analyzed sales performance, distributions, and core business revenue drivers.
3. **Task 3: Deep-Dive Analysis & Interactive Dashboarding** — Designed interactive dashboard reporting systems for cross-functional KPI drill-downs.
4. **Task 4: Data Storytelling & Statistical Validation** — Conducted hypothesis testing ($t$-tests and Chi-Square tests) and statistical validation to back up key business insights.
5. **Task 5: Master Portfolio Synthesis** — Built and structured this central master repository, unifying all sub-repository links, documentation, code bases, and presentation materials.

## 📽️ Final Executive Presentation Deck

The final presentation deck summarizes core metrics, statistical findings, customer segments, and actionable business recommendations.

* 📄 **File Location:** 
  * [Presentation (PDF)](./Final%20Presentation/Customer%20Segmentation%20%26%20Revenue%20Drivers%20Mine.pdf)
  * [Presentation (PPTX)](./Final%20Presentation/Customer%20Segmentation%20%26%20Revenue%20Drivers%20Mine.pptx)
* 💡 **Key Presentation Highlights:**
  * **Core Problem & Dataset:** Analyzed 1,000 transactions generating **₹13.94 Cr** in total gross revenue with an Average Order Value (AOV) of **₹139.40K**.
  * **Product Category Performance:** Electronics (**₹50.78M**) and Education (**₹25.03M**) drive over 51% of total top-line gross revenue.
  * **Customer Segmentation:** Roughly **20%** of premium customer accounts deliver **68%** of cumulative revenues (Pareto Principle).
  * **Statistical Validation:** 
    * *T-Test (Champions vs. At-Risk):* Statistically significant difference in order values ($p < 0.05$). Retaining high-value champions is critical.
    * *Chi-Square Test (Category vs. Region):* No significant dependency ($p > 0.05$), proving purchasing habits are independent of region and marketing can be standardized nationwide.
  * **Strategic Recommendations:** VIP concierge loyalty tiers, automated win-back campaigns for lapsed customers (>90 days), and cross-category bundling (Grocery + Fashion/Education).
